# Hawk's Complete Sound Pack

Release hosting for BeardLib automatic updates.


# About This Sound Pack

This pack almost completely replaces PAYDAY 2's low-quality and, honestly, rather boring reload and weapon-manipulation sounds, while also replacing most weapon firing sounds.

When I originally started working on this pack, it took me about two weeks to put together. During the first week alone, I lost a lot of hours because once you get into the groove of finding where sounds are located, you just don't want to stop.

I decided to release this pack publicly because, at the time, there were essentially no comprehensive SFX replacement packs available, and I wanted to try my hand at making one and see what everyone thought.

I'm an audio guy, so when something doesn't sound right, it constantly nags at me. When you've put as many hours into PAYDAY 2 as I have, those little things eventually start to become really irritating. Once the necessary user tools became available, I set out to replace as many of PAYDAY 2's underwhelming sounds as I could.

Now that the pack has been available for over a year and has matured considerably, I consider it a **complete sound pack**. Things may still be changed or improved over time, but as far as overall content goes, this pack contains by far the most work I've put into it.

# Sound Sources and Credits

Most of the sounds used in this pack come from the almost-dead Source mod **Firearms: Source**.

I used to play the original Firearms back in the Half-Life 1 days, and the team has always produced top-notch weapon animations. More recently, their sound designer has also been creating some excellent weapon audio, so I always felt that if I ever had the opportunity, I would import some of those sounds into PAYDAY 2.

I take **zero credit for creating the original sounds themselves**. My work involved finding, preparing, converting, editing, and implementing them so they could properly function within PAYDAY 2.

I don't have the largest gunshot sound library, so I wasn't able to include absolutely everything I originally wanted, particularly some Battlefield 3 weapon sounds.

Sounds used throughout the pack have come from various sources, including:

* Firearms: Source
* Insurgency
* Red Orchestra: Ostfront 41-45
* Call of Juarez: Gunslinger
* Killing Floor 1 & 2
* Battlefield 3 & 4
* Shadow Warrior
* Medal of Honor: Warfighter
* Rainbow Six: Vegas 2
* DOOM
* Real firearm recordings provided by my Steam friend **Hashimoto Soda**
* Escape From Tarkov

# Development and Feedback

This has largely been a one-man operation.

During the original development of the pack, I was only receiving feedback from two or three Steam friends who helped me identify possible sound errors, inconsistencies, and other problems.

Because of that, please understand that there may occasionally be inconsistencies or things that I have missed. When I originally started this project, I was completely new to this kind of work, and I'm only human. There is only so much one person can find, test, and fix alone.

If you would like to offer help of any kind, feel free to do so.

Likewise, if you have sounds that you think would work well in the game, feel free to suggest them.

I'm not sure how much further I can improve the pack overall, but there is always room for improvement. If I find something that I personally believe will improve the quality of the pack, I will gladly go back and change it.
